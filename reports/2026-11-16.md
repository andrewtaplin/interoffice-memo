## Sector Rotation (GICS)

Defensive sectors showed relative strength amid the risk-off tone. Consumer Staples / Consumer Defensive and Healthcare held up best or posted modest gains in many snapshots, while Technology was the clear laggard (down roughly 2.5–3.3%). Energy, Materials, Industrials, and Consumer Discretionary / Cyclical also lagged. Communication Services was mixed across data providers. The session reflected rotation out of high-growth, AI-exposed names into more defensive areas ahead of key earnings and amid elevated yields.
